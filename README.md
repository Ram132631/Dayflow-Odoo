 Problem Statement

Organizations often manage employee information, attendance, leave requests, and payroll details through manual processes or disconnected systems. This can make HR operations inefficient, increase administrative workload, and make it difficult for employees to access their own HR information.
Employees need a secure platform to manage their profiles, track attendance, apply for leave, and view salary details, while HR/Admin officers need a centralized system to manage employees, monitor attendance, process leave approvals, and manage payroll information.
The key challenge is to create a secure, centralized, and role-based HR management system that simplifies these processes for both employees and HR teams.

 Proposed Solution
Dayflow is a centralized Human Resource Management System that digitizes essential HR operations through a single platform.
The solution provides two role-based experiences:
Employees can securely sign in, manage their profiles, check in/check out, view daily and weekly attendance, apply for paid/sick/unpaid leave, track request status, and view salary information.
Admin/HR Officers can manage employee records, view attendance across employees, review and approve/reject leave requests, add comments, and manage salary structures and payroll information.
Dayflow brings authentication, employee management, attendance tracking, leave workflows, approval management, and payroll visibility together in one platform. It reduces manual HR work, improves employee self-service, centralizes HR information, and provides controlled access to sensitive employee data.
The system can also be extended with the planned email/notification alerts and analytics/reporting dashboard, including attendance reports and salary slips.
